Description
CTS Model 535 is a high performance Temperature Compensated Crystal Oscillator [TCXO] suitable for
applications requiring tight stability, Stratum 3 performance and more. Employing IC technology with Clipped
Sine output and analog temperature compensation engine; coupled with a fundamental quartz crystal M535 has
excellent stability and low jitter/phase noise performance.
