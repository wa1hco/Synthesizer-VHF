Description
CTS Model 580 is a high performance Temperature Compensated Crystal Oscillator [TCXO] suitable for
applications requiring Stratum 3 performance. Employing analog IC technology with clipped sine output and high
order temperature compensation engine; coupled with a fundamental quartz crystal M580 has excellent stability
and low jitter/phase noise performance.
